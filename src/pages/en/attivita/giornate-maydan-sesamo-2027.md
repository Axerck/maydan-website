The call for papers for the third edition of the study days of *Maydan: rivista sui mondi arabi, semitici e islamici* and SeSaMO (Società per gli Studi sul Medio Oriente) is now open. The study days will take place on **18 and 19 February 2027** at the University of Naples L'Orientale.

The study days are aimed at PhD students, graduates and final-year master's students, and are designed to support young scholars in the early stages of their research and publishing career. Each presentation will be part of a thematic panel, with a discussant offering structured feedback on the papers. The study days are the main selection venue for the contributions to the seventh issue of Maydan.

Proposals take the form of a long abstract (800–1000 words, in Italian or English) and must be submitted anonymously, in .pdf and .docx format, by **23 October 2026** to [submission.rivista@maydan.it](mailto:submission.rivista@maydan.it). Full details on how to apply are in the call (in Italian).

## Timeline

- Deadline for proposals: 23/10/2026
- Notification of acceptance: 07/12/2026
- Submission of presentations: 29/01/2027
- Study days in Naples: 18–19/02/2027
- Deadline for full papers: 20/04/2027
- Peer-review outcome: 21/06/2027
- Acceptance of revised papers: 13/09/2027
- Publication of the seventh issue: December 2027
